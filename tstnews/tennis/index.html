Tennis: Davidovich Fokina wins the Shanghai Masters

Champion · final Sun 18 Oct

Alejandro Davidovich Fokina is a Masters 1000 champion. The Spaniard, seeded 25th, beat his compatriot Rafael Jodar, the 20-year-old seeded 12th, in the Shanghai final on Sunday 18 October. The ATP page shows the score as 16 76(5) 64, which reads as 1–6, 7–6(5), 6–4 (separators missing on the page).

Both men reached the final by beating the seeds in the semi-finals, which means top seed Alexander Zverev and Daniil Medvedev went out in the last four; semi-final scores were not found. In the doubles final, Austria's Lucas Miedler and Neil Oberleitner beat Francisco Cabral and JJ Tracy 6–3, 6–4.

The ATP page's ranking list is still dated 5 October, so the post-Shanghai rankings were not available. Spanish media reported earlier that Zverev was on course to finish 2026 as world number one.

If you're new to this

The men's tour has four levels. The four Grand Slams (Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open) are on top, followed by nine Masters 1000 events including Shanghai, then smaller tournaments.

A match is played in sets, and a set in games. Within a game, points go 15, 30, 40, then game, and at 40-40 (deuce) a player must win two points in a row. Most tour matches are the best of three sets, and Slams are the best of five for men.

Rankings are based on points won over the previous 52 weeks, so injuries hurt twice: a missed event means missed points and a falling ranking.

Tennis: Chwalinska wins the Wuhan Open (WTA 1000)

Champion · final Sun 18 Oct

Maja Chwalinska has won the Wuhan Open. The Pole, seeded 16th, beat Britain's Sonay Kartal in the final after coming from behind, according to Flashscore headlines (the match page and score were not read). On the way she beat Poland's Iga Swiatek in the semi-final; Flashscore's headline says Swiatek was injured.

Kartal had been the tournament's story: she beat 5th seed Elina Svitolina in the quarter-finals. Score details for the semi-finals and final were not found.

  • Champion: Maja Chwalinska (final v Sonay Kartal, comeback win, per Flashscore)
  • Semi-final: Chwalinska d. Swiatek (Flashscore headline)

If you're new to this

Wuhan is one of the WTA's 1000-level events, the tour's second-biggest tier after the four Grand Slams. The champion gets 1,000 ranking points.

Rankings count the best results from the last 52 weeks, so a first-round exit costs a seed her chance to add points, while the 1000 events in Asia come at the end of the season as players chase a place at the WTA Finals.
