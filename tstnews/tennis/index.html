Davis Cup: Italy make the final; Britain face Spain for the other place

Davis Cup Final 8 · 27–28 Nov

Italy needed the last rubber, and then had to come from a set down in it. Lorenzo Musetti and Andrea Vavassori beat Jiri Lehecka and Tomas Machac 3–6, 6–1, 7–6(4) on Friday to beat Czechia 2–1 and put the holders into a fourth straight final, all without the injured Jannik Sinner.

Now it is Britain's turn. Their semi-final with Spain starts at 11am UK (6am Toronto) on Saturday, and no result was in when this edition was prepared. Carlos Alcaraz is in Spain's nominated squad; whether he plays was not confirmed. The winners meet Italy on Sunday at 2pm UK (9am Toronto).

  • Italy 2–1 Czechia (Fri 27 Nov)
  • Musetti d. Mensik 6–3, 6–4
  • Lehecka d. Cobolli 6–2, 1–6, 6–3
  • Musetti/Vavassori d. Lehecka/Machac 3–6, 6–1, 7–6(4)

If you're new to this

The Davis Cup is men's tennis's international team competition, with players representing their countries rather than only themselves.

At the Final 8 each tie is decided over three matches: two singles and then a doubles. The first country to win two goes through, so the doubles matters most when the singles are shared.

Eight nations play a straight knockout in Bologna from 24 to 29 November. Earlier in the week Britain beat Germany 2–1 and Spain beat Austria 2–0.

Tennis: Alcaraz wins the ATP Finals

Turin · final Sun 22 Nov · background

Carlos Alcaraz ended the individual season with a new trophy for his shelf. The Spaniard beat top seed Alexander Zverev 6–2, 7–6(7) in Turin on Sunday 22 November, his first title at the Nitto ATP Finals, according to the ATP Tour.

Theo Arribage and Albano Olivetti of France won the doubles on their event debut.

  • Singles final (Sun 22 Nov): Alcaraz d. Zverev 6-2, 7-6(7)
  • Doubles final: Arribage/Olivetti d. Arevalo/Pavic 3-6, 7-6(2), 10-6

If you're new to this

The ATP Finals is the year-end tournament for the eight best men's singles players of the season, played indoors rather than as a knockout from the start.

The eight are split into two groups of four; everyone plays the other three in their group, and the top two from each group go through to the semi-finals.

Qualifying is based on ranking points won over the season.
