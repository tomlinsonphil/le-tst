Formula 1: champion Antonelli sweeps Friday in Qatar

Qatar GP · practice done, qualifying Sat

Winning the title has not slowed Kimi Antonelli. The Mercedes driver was quickest in both Friday practice sessions at Lusail, finishing second practice in 1:23.077, just 0.127 seconds ahead of team-mate George Russell, with Lando Norris third.

It was a scrappy evening. Pierre Gasly's spin into the gravel brought one of two red flags, Charles Leclerc also spun, and Lewis Hamilton's Ferrari lost power late on. Final practice is Saturday at 2.30pm UK (9.30am Toronto) and qualifying at 6pm (1pm). The 57-lap race is Sunday at 4pm UK (11am Toronto).

  • FP1: 1 Antonelli 1:23.217, 2 Russell, 3 Hadjar
  • FP2: 1 Antonelli 1:23.077, 2 Russell +0.127, 3 Norris +0.445, 4 Piastri +0.472, 5 Verstappen +0.499

If you're new to this

Formula 1 is the top level of single-seater motor racing. Teams, called constructors, each run two cars.

The top ten in a Grand Prix score points, with 25 for the winner. One championship is for drivers and one for teams, which add up both cars' scores.

Pole position is first place on the starting grid, earned in qualifying. A lock-up is when a wheel stops turning under heavy braking.

With Antonelli already champion, much of the interest now is in the fight for the places behind him.

MotoGP: new champion Marquez tops Friday at the Valencia finale

Valencia · 27–29 Nov

Marc Marquez has the crown and still wants the stopwatch. Six days after eighth place in Portugal sealed his eighth premier-class title, level with Giacomo Agostini, the Ducati rider set a lap record of 1:28.483 in Friday practice at Valencia.

Marco Bezzecchi, the Portuguese race winner, was 0.247 seconds back. Sunday's race ends the season and, according to Crash.net, settles the constructors' title between Aprilia and Ducati.

  • After Portugal: Marquez 439, Martin 396, Bezzecchi 394
  • Friday practice: Marquez, Bezzecchi, Ogura

If you're new to this

MotoGP is the top world championship for motorcycle road racing. Riders score points at each round and the highest total wins the title.

As in Formula 1, there is also a championship for manufacturers, so a race can still matter after the riders' title is settled.

Practice sessions let riders tune their bikes. Their lap times also help decide who goes straight into the final part of qualifying.
