Squash: Siow breaks home hearts in a 77-minute semi-final

CAS International · 27–28 Nov

Asim Khan clawed back from two games down, only to see it slip in the fifth. Malaysia's sixth seed Yee Xian Siow beat the Pakistani fourth seed 11–8, 11–8, 5–11, 9–11, 11–5 on Friday in 77 minutes.

Waiting for him is compatriot Joachim Chuah, the second seed, who needed only 27 minutes to beat Egypt's Marwan Assal 3–0. The all-Malaysian final was set for Saturday at 3pm; no result had been found.

If you're new to this

Squash is played in an enclosed court, with players taking turns to hit a small rubber ball against the front wall.

Matches are usually best of five games, so 3–2 means it went the distance.

The PSA is the body that runs the professional tour. Tournaments come in tiers; this one is in the Copper tier, with US$40,000 in prize money.

A 'seed' is a player's ranking position within a tournament draw.

Squash: top seed Gawad and Crouin meet in a Hong Kong final

HKFC Milwaukee Open · 28 Nov

Hong Kong has one final with the top two seeds and another with a gatecrasher. Karim Gawad beat Declan James 3–0 and Victor Crouin saw off Youssef Ibrahim 3–0, taking the last game 18–16.

In the women's draw, fourth seed Marina Stefanoni outlasted second seed Tinne Gilis 3–2, 16–14 in the fifth, to face top seed Fayrouz Aboelkheir. Neither final result was available. The bigger Hong Kong Squash Open follows from Monday.

If you're new to this

This is a Silver-level event on the PSA professional tour, with US$96,500 listed for the men's and women's draws.

Squash matches are usually best of five games. A game that finishes 18–16 has gone well past the normal finishing line, because a player must win by two clear points.

Seeds are the players ranked highest in a draw. They are placed apart so that they cannot meet in the early rounds.
