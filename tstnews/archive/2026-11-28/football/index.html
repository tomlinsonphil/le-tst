Premier League: Gibbs-White double sinks Chelsea

Matchday 12 · 27 Nov

The City Ground got a five-goal Friday night. Igor Jesus volleyed Nottingham Forest ahead after five minutes, Morgan Gibbs-White added a penalty and then a second goal, and two strikes from half-time substitute Emmanuel Emegha could not rescue Chelsea, who lost 3–2.

Forest rise to 14th for now. Chelsea sit eighth, and NBC Sports notes they have won only one of their last five league games. Today brings Manchester United v Brentford and Leeds v Coventry at 3pm UK (10am Toronto), then Ipswich v Aston Villa at 5.30pm (12.30pm Toronto). Arsenal host Manchester City on Sunday at 4.30pm (11.30am Toronto).

If you're new to this

The Premier League is the top division of English football. Its 20 clubs each play one another twice, home and away, for 38 matches.

A win is worth three points and a draw one. If clubs are level, goal difference (goals scored minus goals conceded) helps separate them.

The champions win the title, the top places earn spots in European competitions and the bottom three drop to the Championship.

Bournemouth: perfect in Europe, off to Fulham next

Followed club

Five Europa League games, five wins. Thursday's 2–1 victory in Vigo, achieved with ten men through Amine Adli and substitute Rayan, leaves Bournemouth top on 15 points, two clear of Juventus and Ferencváros.

In the Premier League they are fifth with 18 points from 11 games. The run ahead: Fulham away on Sunday (2.05pm UK, 9.05am Toronto), Brighton at home on Wednesday 2 December (8.15pm) and Hull at home on Saturday 5 December (3pm). Viktoria Plzeň visit in Europe on 10 December, and a Carabao Cup quarter-final at Liverpool is due in the week of 14 December.

If you're new to this

The Europa League is the second tier of European club football, below the Champions League.

It begins with a single league table of 36 clubs. The top eight go straight to the last 16, ninth to 24th play off, and the rest go out.

Wikipedia notes this is the first time Bournemouth, a club from a seaside town, have qualified for European football.

After matchday 5

If you're new to this

The Champions League is Europe's top club competition. It starts with one big league phase in which each club plays eight different opponents.

All 36 clubs sit in a single table. The top eight go straight to the last 16, ninth to 24th play off for the remaining places, and the rest are eliminated.

'Matchday 5' means the fifth of those eight rounds.

If you're new to this

Ligue 1 is France's top division, with 18 clubs playing 34 matches each. A win is worth three points and a draw one.

The leading clubs qualify for European competitions, while those at the bottom face relegation or a play-off.

Paris Saint-Germain are the defending champions, having won five titles in a row. Paris FC are a separate club from the same city.

If you're new to this

The Lionesses is the nickname of England's senior women's football team.

A play-off is an extra knockout stage for teams that have not yet secured a place at a tournament; this one leads towards the 2027 Women's World Cup.

The FA Cup is open to clubs far down the English football pyramid, which is why small teams can draw much bigger ones.
