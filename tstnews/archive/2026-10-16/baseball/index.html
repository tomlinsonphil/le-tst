Blue Jays

Season over

October baseball is going on without Toronto. The Blue Jays were eliminated with a 4–2 loss to the Orioles on 23 September, as reported by CP24 and MLB.com, so the reigning American League champions are watching from home.

The league championship series are well under way. In the National League the Brewers lead the Dodgers 3–0, after beating the Padres in four games in the division series. In the American League the Guardians and Rays are level at 1–1. The World Series is due to open on Friday 23 October.

If you're new to this

Baseball is played between two teams of nine across nine innings. In each innings one team bats and the other fields, and a half-innings ends after three outs. The pitcher throws to the batter, who tries to hit the ball and run round four bases to score.

Major League Baseball has 30 teams in two leagues. Twelve teams make the postseason. After the wild-card round come the division series, the league championship series and the World Series.

The regular season is 162 games, so single results matter less than in football and the best teams still lose about 60 games.
