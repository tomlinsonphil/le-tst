Tennis: the Shanghai Masters

Quarter-finals 15–16 Oct

Alexander Zverev is through to the last eight in Shanghai and reports say he will finish 2026 as world number one. Carlos Alcaraz was knocked out on Monday by Belgian Zizou Bergs, 7–5, 7–6(4), which, according to Spanish reports, ends his chance of catching Zverev. Jannik Sinner, still top on 11,000 points, is reported to be out until 2027 with physical problems.

The quarter-finals are Zverev v Alex de Minaur and Arthur Gea v Alejandro Davidovich Fokina on Thursday 15 October, then Rafael Jodar v Casper Ruud and Daniil Medvedev v Tommy Paul on Friday 16 October. On Wednesday Medvedev beat Stefanos Tsitsipas 6–4, 7–5, Paul beat Bergs 7–6(5), 6–4, Jodar came back again to beat Botic van de Zandschulp, and Felix Auger-Aliassime retired with a knee injury while trailing Ruud 5–2.

It is a bruising week for the big names. Ben Shelton lost to Gea on 11 October, and Novak Djokovic went out to Hubert Hurkacz on 9 October. The final is on Sunday 18 October.

If you're new to this

The men's tour has four levels. The four Grand Slams (Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open) are on top, followed by nine Masters 1000 events including Shanghai, then smaller tournaments.

A match is played in sets, and a set in games. Within a game, points go 15, 30, 40, then game, and at 40-40 (deuce) a player must win two points in a row. Most tour matches are the best of three sets, and Slams are the best of five for men.

Rankings are based on points won over the previous 52 weeks, so injuries hurt twice: a missed event means missed points and a falling ranking.

Tennis: the Wuhan Open (WTA 1000)

12–18 Oct

Aryna Sabalenka is out in her first match. The top seed lost 3–6, 6–4, 7–6(5) to American Ann Li on Wednesday after saving nine match points from 2–5 down in the decider, and Li took her tenth chance after 2 hours 27 minutes. Second seed Jessica Pegula also fell, 2–6, 7–6(7), 6–3 to Jelena Ostapenko.

Iga Swiatek (7th seed) won 6–2, 6–1 over Magdalena Frech and plays Ashlyn Krueger next. Karolina Muchova saved her tournament in two tiebreaks against Caty McNally, and world No 170 Sonay Kartal upset ninth seed Belinda Bencic 6–0, 7–5.

Elena Rybakina, the world No 1, and defending champion Coco Gauff pulled out before the draw, as did Anisimova, Keys and Osaka according to a 9 October entry-list report.

If you're new to this

Wuhan is one of the WTA's 1000-level events, the tour's second-biggest tier after the four Grand Slams. The champion gets 1,000 ranking points.

Rankings count the best results from the last 52 weeks, so a first-round exit costs a seed her chance to add points, while the 1000 events in Asia come at the end of the season as players chase a place at the WTA Finals.
