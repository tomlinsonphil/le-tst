Blue Jays land Kaima Taira, then go shopping

Off-season · 24–25 Nov

Toronto has opened the winter with a pitcher. On Tuesday 24 November the Blue Jays agreed a five-year, US$80m deal with Japanese right-hander Kaima Taira. MLB.com says the club went 79–83 in 2026 and hit the fewest home runs in the majors, so a bat is also on the list.

Rumours link the Jays with outfielders Seiya Suzuki and Randy Arozarena and with more pitching, including a possible reunion with Kevin Gausman. Those are reports, not signings.

If you're new to this

Baseball is played between two teams of nine across nine innings. In each innings one team bats and the other fields, and a half-innings ends after three outs. The pitcher throws to the batter, who tries to hit the ball and run round four bases to score.

Major League Baseball has 30 teams in two leagues. Twelve teams make the postseason. After the wild-card round come the division series, the league championship series and the World Series.

The regular season is 162 games, so single results matter less than in football and the best teams still lose about 60 games.

In the off-season, players whose contracts have ended can sign with any club as free agents. Teams can also trade players with each other.
