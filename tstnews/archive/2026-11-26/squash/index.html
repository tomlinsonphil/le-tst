Squash: Hossam topples the top seed in Islamabad; Asal and Orfi still No. 1

PSA Tour · 23–26 Nov

The upset of the week in Islamabad came on Wednesday, when Hazem Hossam stunned top seed Noor Zaman 3–2 at the CAS International. Hossam's run then ended in a five-game quarter-final against Yee Xian Siow, who is listed to play Asim Khan in Friday's semi-final; the final is on Saturday.

The world rankings dated 23 November are led by Mostafa Asal among the men and Amina Orfi among the women.

If you're new to this

Squash is played in an enclosed court, with players taking turns to hit a small rubber ball against the front wall.

Matches are usually best of five games, so 3–2 means it went the distance.

The PSA is the body that runs the professional tour. Tournaments come in tiers; the Islamabad event is in the Copper tier, with US$40,000 in prize money.

A 'seed' is a player's ranking position within a tournament draw.
