Davis Cup: Britain win the decider to reach the semi-finals

Davis Cup Final 8 · 26 Nov

It came down to the doubles, and Henry Patten and Neal Skupski delivered, beating Kevin Krawietz and Tim Pütz 6–2, 7–6(6) to send Great Britain past Germany 2–1. Cameron Norrie had beaten Jan-Lennard Struff before Alexander Zverev levelled against Arthur Fery.

Next are Spain, who beat Austria 2–0 without picking Carlos Alcaraz for singles; Rafael Jódar came back from a set down. Britain's semi-final is on Saturday. Italy and Czechia meet in the other. Canada went out to Czechia on Tuesday.

If you're new to this

The Davis Cup is men's tennis's international team competition, with players representing their countries.

At the Final 8 each tie is decided over three matches: two singles and one doubles. The first country to win two goes through.

Eight nations play a straight knockout in Bologna from 24 to 29 November. Canada won the competition once before.

Tennis: Alcaraz wins the Paris Masters

Rolex Paris Masters · final Sun 8 Nov

Carlos Alcaraz is the Rolex Paris Masters champion. The second seed from Spain beat American third seed Ben Shelton 7–6(5), 7–5 in the final on Sunday 8 November, according to the ATP Tour's results page.

The week had set the pair up on Saturday: Alcaraz beat Casper Ruud 6–4, 6–4 and Shelton came through a three-setter against home hope Luca Van Assche, 5–7, 6–3, 6–4. The ATP page did not give the doubles final, and the post-Paris world rankings had not been read, so the ATP top 10 below is still the 19 Oct list.

If you're new to this

The Rolex Paris Masters is the last Masters 1000 event of the ATP season, played indoors in Paris from 2 to 8 November 2026.

A Masters 1000 is the tier just below the Grand Slams, with 1000 ranking points for the champion.

A qualifier reached the main draw through a preliminary event; a lucky loser is a beaten qualifier who gets a late place when someone withdraws.

Tennis: Alcaraz wins the ATP Finals

Turin · final Sun 22 Nov

Carlos Alcaraz is the Nitto ATP Finals champion. The second seed from Spain beat top seed Alexander Zverev of Germany 6-2, 7-6(7) in Sunday's final in Turin, taking his first title at the season finale, according to the ATP Tour.

The season finale has its eight. The ATP Tour lists the singles qualifiers for the Nitto ATP Finals as Alexander Zverev, Carlos Alcaraz, Ben Shelton, Daniil Medvedev, Flavio Cobolli, Arthur Fils, Frances Tiafoe and Rafael Jodar.

The event runs 15–22 November in Turin, in two groups (John McEnroe and Ivan Lendl), and the draw is on Thursday 12 November at about 12:45 pm CET.

Update, Thursday 19 Nov in Turin: Alexander Zverev beat Arthur Fils 6-4 7-6(6) and Ben Shelton beat Rafael Jodar 7-6(8) 7-5, sending Shelton into his first Finals semi-final. The tournament runs 15-22 Nov at the Inalpi Arena. Group tables were not on the page read.

Update, Saturday 21 Nov: Alexander Zverev beat Daniil Medvedev 7-6(0) 6-4 and Carlos Alcaraz beat Ben Shelton 3-6 6-3 7-6(1) in the semi-finals, so Zverev and Alcaraz contest the singles final. Doubles final: Arevalo/Pavic v Arribage/Olivetti.

Doubles final, Sun 22 Nov: sixth seeds Theo Arribage and Albano Olivetti of France beat fourth seeds Marcelo Arevalo and Mate Pavic 3-6, 7-6(2), 10-6 on their event debut.

  • Singles final (Sun 22 Nov): Alcaraz d. Zverev 6-2, 7-6(7)

If you're new to this

The ATP Finals is the year-end tournament for the eight best men's singles players of the season, played indoors rather than as a knockout from the start.

The eight are split into two groups of four; everyone plays the other three in their group, and the top two from each group go through to the semi-finals.

Qualifying is based on ranking points won over the season.

If you're new to this

The men's tour has four levels. The four Grand Slams (Australian Open, French Open, Wimbledon, US Open) are on top, followed by nine Masters 1000 events including Shanghai, then smaller tournaments.

A match is played in sets, and a set in games. Within a game, points go 15, 30, 40, then game, and at 40-40 (deuce) a player must win two points in a row. Most tour matches are the best of three sets, and Slams are the best of five for men.

Rankings are based on points won over the previous 52 weeks, so injuries hurt twice: a missed event means missed points and a falling ranking.

Tennis: Chwalinska wins the Wuhan Open (WTA 1000)

Champion · final Sun 18 Oct

Maja Chwalinska has won the Wuhan Open. The Pole, seeded 16th, beat Britain's Sonay Kartal in the final after coming from behind, according to Flashscore headlines (the match page and score were not read). If you're new to this

Wuhan is one of the WTA's 1000-level events, the tour's second-biggest tier after the four Grand Slams. The champion gets 1,000 ranking points.

Rankings count the best results from the last 52 weeks, so a first-round exit costs a seed her chance to add points, while the 1000 events in Asia come at the end of the season as players chase a place at the WTA Finals.
