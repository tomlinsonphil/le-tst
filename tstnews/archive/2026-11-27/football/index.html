If you're new to this

The Premier League is the top division of English football. Its 20 clubs each play one another twice, home and away, for 38 matches.

A win is worth three points and a draw one. If clubs are level, goal difference (goals scored minus goals conceded) helps separate them.

The champions win the title, the top places earn spots in European competitions and the bottom three drop to the Championship.

Arsenal against Manchester City matters because both have been recent title contenders; points taken from a rival count twice, in effect.

If you're new to this

The Champions League is Europe's top club competition. It starts with one big league phase in which each club plays eight different opponents.

All clubs sit in a single table. The best go straight to the last 16, the next group play off for the remaining places, and the rest are eliminated.

'Matchday 5' means the fifth of those eight rounds.

Tenerife's Jesus de Miguel dies after on-field collision

Spain · 3 Nov

Tenerife forward Jesus de Miguel, 30, has died after a collision in the club's Spanish second-division match against Andorra on Monday 2 November, Al Jazeera reports.

The game was heading for a 2-2 draw when, seven minutes into added time, goalkeeper Jesus Owono's knee appeared to strike de Miguel's chest; Owono was shown a red card, the match was abandoned and players were in tears. Reports say he suffered a cardiac arrest in an ambulance.

Tenerife announced his death on Tuesday: 'Broken with pain, with a lump in our throat and unable to believe that this is true.' His family has asked for privacy.

Update from Al Jazeera on 4 Nov: Tenerife declared six days of mourning, Spanish domestic matches were postponed and UEFA held a minute's silence at Champions League games. Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono said he 'never intended to hurt anyone'; local police opened an investigation, though criminal proceedings are considered unlikely.

If you're new to this

The Premier League is England's top division, with 20 clubs. Each plays every other club twice, home and away, for 38 matches. A win earns 3 points, a draw 1 and a defeat 0. Ties in points are split by goal difference, then goals scored.

The bottom three clubs are relegated to the Championship, and three clubs promoted from there take their place. This promotion and relegation system runs through all of English football, down through League One, League Two and the non-league levels.

The top finishers qualify for European competition the following season, so the table matters at the top as well as the bottom. The season runs from August to May.

If you're new to this

Ligue 1 is France's top division, with 18 clubs playing 34 matches each. Scoring works like England: 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw.

The champion and runners-up reach the Champions League. Clubs in the bottom places face relegation or a play-off against clubs from Ligue 2.

Money is the main story. PSG's budget has dwarfed the others since Qatar's investment in 2011, which is why other clubs such as Monaco, Lille, Marseille and Lyon are watched as challengers.

If you're new to this

English football has a deep pyramid. The EFL runs the Championship, League One and League Two, with 24 clubs each, and below that sits the National League.

The FA Cup is open to the whole pyramid, from the Premier League to semi-professional clubs, which produces upsets. The EFL's own knockout is the Carabao Cup, which starts in August.

In France, the Coupe de France is also open to amateur clubs, and small teams routinely meet professional ones in early rounds.

La Liga: Barcelona 6-1 Alaves

Spain · 31 Oct

Barcelona hammered Alaves 6-1 at the Camp Nou on Saturday 31 October, with Raphinha and Lamine Yamal each scoring twice, plus Fermin Lopez and substitute Anthony Gordon; Mariano Diaz replied for Alaves.

The win put Barcelona back on top of La Liga, two points clear of Atletico Madrid, who won 2-0 at Levante. Real Madrid are third. It was a bounce-back after the Champions League loss to PSG and the 2-1 El Clasico defeat the previous weekend.
