Raptors

Preseason 2-1 · opener 21 Oct

Kawhi Leonard's home re-debut was a rout. The Raptors beat the Knicks 148–98 in preseason on Tuesday 13 October, leading 86–47 at half. Leonard scored a game-high 21 points in 17 minutes (4-of-8 from three) and sat the second half; Scottie Barnes added 19 and RJ Barrett 17, per CP24. Toronto are 2-1 in preseason, the Knicks 0-4.

Two exhibitions remain: Thursday at New York and Friday against the Pistons in Detroit. The real thing starts Wednesday 21 October at home to the Chicago Bulls (tip-off time not given). Orlando follows on 30 October in the NBA Cup opener.

If you're new to this

The NBA has 30 teams and an 82-game regular season. Each team has five players on court. A shot scores 2 points, or 3 from beyond the arc, and a free throw scores 1.

The top six teams in each conference make the playoffs outright, and seeds 7 to 10 play a "play-in" tournament for the last two places. The playoffs are four rounds, each a best-of-seven.

There is a salary cap and a draft, which keeps spending closer than in most sports.
