Formula 1: Norris wins in Sao Paulo; Antonelli already champion

Sao Paulo result Sun 8 Nov · Antonelli champion since 1 Nov

Lando Norris converted pole into victory at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix on Sunday 8 November. On the order shown by racingnews365.com he finished 14.6 seconds clear of Max Verstappen, with Red Bull's Isack Hadjar third, Mercedes pair George Russell and Kimi Antonelli fourth and fifth, and Oscar Piastri sixth.

The same page carries a headline saying the results were adjusted after a Ferrari time penalty, so the order below is provisional; the Ferrari places (Leclerc eighth, Hamilton ninth) in particular may have changed. Drivers' and constructors' points after Brazil were not verified, so those tables still show the position after Mexico City.

The title was settled a week earlier: Kimi Antonelli clinched the world championship in Mexico City on 1 November, at 20 years and 68 days the youngest champion in Formula 1 history and the first Italian since Alberto Ascari in 1953.

If you're new to this

Formula 1 is a world championship for single-seater racing cars. Each season has around two dozen race weekends across the world, called Grands Prix. Ten teams used to compete, and the 2026 grid has 11, with Audi and Cadillac among the newer names.

Drivers score points in each race: 25 for a win, then 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 and 1 for places two to ten. Some weekends also include a shorter sprint race with a smaller points scale. Points add up across the season, and the driver with the most is world champion.

A separate constructors' championship adds up both of a team's drivers' points. Teams care about it because the prize money depends on it.

The sport changed its rules for 2026, with new engines and chassis regulations, which is one reason the order at the front looks different from recent years.

MotoGP: Marquez wins in Malaysia

Sepang · 1 Nov

Marc Marquez (Ducati) won the Malaysian Grand Prix at Sepang by more than four seconds, ahead of Pedro Acosta (KTM) and his brother Alex Marquez (Gresini), who started on pole. He now has 399 points to Jorge Martin's 355.

Martin missed the race with a fractured wrist from a crash at Phillip Island and conceded his title bid is essentially over. Marquez could seal an eighth title at next weekend's Qatar Grand Prix if he extends his lead to 74 points, with two rounds left.

  • Standings: Marquez 399, Martin 355
  • Next: Qatar GP, Lusail, next weekend

If you're new to this

MotoGP is the premier world championship for motorcycle road racing. Riders score points at each Grand Prix and the highest total at season's end is champion.

Ducati, KTM, Gresini (a Ducati-supplied team) and Aprilia are among the teams on the grid.
