If you're new to this

Squash is played by two players in a four-walled court, taking turns to hit a small rubber ball against the front wall above a line. The ball may hit other walls first but must not bounce twice before being returned.

Games go to 11 points with a point scored on every rally (point-a-rally), and a match is usually the best of five. A player must win by two clear points.

The professional circuit is run by the Professional Squash Association (PSA). Squash joined the Olympic programme for Los Angeles 2028.
