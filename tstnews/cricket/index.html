Cricket: Overton's five puts England ahead

3rd T20I · 27 Nov

Jamie Overton tore the heart out of Australia at the Gabba. His career-best 5 for 12, with 3 for 16 from Liam Dawson, bowled the hosts out for 108 in 16.5 overs.

The DLS method then set a revised target of 93 from 16 overs, and England needed only 8.5, reaching 93 for 3 with Jordan Cox unbeaten on 33 and Aneurin Donald making 32. They lead 2–1 and can take the series in Sydney on Sunday (8.15am UK, 3.15am Toronto).

If you're new to this

Cricket is a bat-and-ball game between two teams of 11. The batting side scores runs; the bowling side tries to get batters out, which is called taking wickets.

In Twenty20, each team faces at most 20 overs, and an over is six balls, so games take about three hours.

'Won by eight wickets' means the side batting second passed the target having lost only two batters.

When a match is interrupted, often by weather, a formula known as DLS can reset the score the chasing side needs.

Tests: South Africa sweep; India in trouble

23–28 Nov

South Africa needed only three days at Centurion, beating Bangladesh by an innings and 219 runs for a 2–0 sweep. Wiaan Mulder made 100 and took eight wickets, including 7 for 31, while Marques Ackerman hit 150.

In Christchurch, India closed day two on 116 for 6 in reply to New Zealand's 356, still 40 short of avoiding the follow-on. Akash Deep had earlier taken 5 for 99. The first Test in Wellington was drawn.

If you're new to this

A Test match is cricket's longest form, played over up to five days with two innings for each side.

Winning 'by an innings' means one team scored more in a single innings than the other managed in two.

The follow-on lets a side with a big first-innings lead make its opponents bat again immediately.
