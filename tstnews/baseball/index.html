Blue Jays reportedly land Jazz Chisholm Jr.

Reported deal · 27 Nov

Toronto is not waiting around this winter. On Friday evening, reporters said the Blue Jays had a deal with second baseman Jazz Chisholm Jr., a one-year contract with a player option for 2028. MLB Trade Rumors puts the guarantee at US$34.5m. No club announcement had been seen.

The bet is on a bounce-back. Chisholm hit .231 with 19 home runs for the Yankees in 2026, a down year after 31 homers and 31 stolen bases in 2025. If he turns down the second year, Toronto would hold a US$40m option.

If you're new to this

The second baseman is one of the four infielders, playing between first and second base, where quick hands matter for turning two outs on one hit.

A player option lets the player decide whether to stay for another year. A club option gives that choice to the team.

In the off-season, players whose contracts have ended are free agents and can sign with any club. Deals are often reported by journalists before the team confirms them.

The three batting figures are batting average, on-base percentage and slugging percentage, which rewards extra-base hits.

If you're new to this

Baseball is played between two teams of nine across nine innings. In each innings one team bats and the other fields, and a half-innings ends after three outs. The pitcher throws to the batter, who tries to hit the ball and run round four bases to score.

Major League Baseball has 30 teams in two leagues. Twelve teams make the postseason. After the wild-card round come the division series, the league championship series and the World Series.

The regular season is 162 games, so single results matter less than in football and the best teams still lose about 60 games.

In the off-season, players whose contracts have ended can sign with any club as free agents. Teams can also trade players with each other.
