If you're new to this

Two teams of 15 play two 40-minute halves. You can run with the ball or kick it, but you can only pass it sideways or backwards. A try, where a player grounds the ball over the opponents' line, scores 5 points. A conversion kick adds 2, and a penalty kick or drop goal scores 3.

The game is built on set pieces. A scrum restarts play after minor infringements, with the forwards pushing against each other. A lineout restarts play after the ball goes out of play, with a jumper lifted to catch the throw.

The Nations Championship is new in 2026. Twelve national teams sit in two pools, a European one (England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland, Wales) and a Rest of the World one (Argentina, Australia, Fiji, Japan, New Zealand, South Africa). Each team plays every team in the other pool once, across a July window and a November window, and the top sides meet in finals at Twickenham.

A win earns 4 points, and bonus points reward scoring four or more tries or losing narrowly.

Rugby union: Bledisloe Cup

Bledisloe won by NZ

The All Blacks have kept the Bledisloe Cup, and they did it loudly. New Zealand beat Australia 50–19 at Eden Park, Auckland, on Saturday 10 October, running in tries through Peter Lakai, Tupou Vaa'i, Cam Roigard, captain Codie Taylor, Leroy Carter and Damian McKenzie. It was the 24th straight year the Cup stayed in New Zealand.

The Wallabies were only three points down at half-time, after tries from Tom Wright and Ben Donaldson, before the home side pulled away. The second Test, with the trophy already decided, is at Stadium Australia in Sydney on Saturday 17 October.

In Europe, the Champions Cup pool stage begins with Round 1 on 16, 17 and 18 October, the first of four pool rounds before the knockouts and a final in Lyon on 22 May 2027. I did not find confirmed match times or results for it today.

  • Bledisloe Test 1: New Zealand 50, Australia 19 (Eden Park, 10 Oct)
  • Test 2: Sydney, 17 Oct
  • Champions Cup Round 1: 16 to 18 Oct; final 22 May 2027, Lyon
