Ireland pull away from Australia to take fifth

27 Nov · Twickenham

Ireland were 'scrappy at times', in Sam Prendergast's words, but ruthless where it counted. Robbie Henshaw, Nick Timoney and Rob Baloucoune crossed, James McNabney added two late tries and Australia were beaten 38–14. Jeremy Williams and Kalani Thomas replied.

The opener was far tighter. Italy led Fiji 17–0, fell behind 21–20, and won 23–21 through Tommaso Allan's penalty, ending a seven-game losing run.

England chase a second win over the All Blacks

28 Nov · third-place play-off

A week after a record 37–12 win, England get New Zealand again at 4.40pm UK (11.40am Toronto). Steve Borthwick has made five changes, with Greg Fisilau earning his first international start in the back row, and Freddie Steward, Tommy Freeman, Ellis Genge and Luke Cowan-Dickie also coming in.

First up, at 1.10pm (8.10am Toronto), Wales face Japan with Dafydd Jenkins taking over as captain among five changes.

France and South Africa line up for the title

Final · 29 Nov

The first Nations Championship final has a score to settle. France edged South Africa 21–19 in Paris two weeks earlier, and they meet again at 4.40pm UK on Sunday (11.40am Toronto, 5.40pm in France), with Antoine Dupont captaining France.

Siya Kolisi leads the back-to-back world champions, with Eben Etzebeth and Malcolm Marx on the bench. ESPN reports that coach Rassie Erasmus has been given a six-game ban for criticising match officials after the Paris game.
