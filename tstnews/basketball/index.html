Barnes pours in 34 as Raptors rout the Nets, then lose out on the Cup

Final · Fri 27 Nov

Scottie Barnes gave Toronto the night it needed. He scored 34 points, going 10-for-10 at the line, as the Raptors beat Brooklyn 124–100 on Friday. Immanuel Quickley added a triple-double: 24 points, 14 assists and 10 rebounds.

The sting came minutes later. Toronto finished 3–1 in NBA Cup group play and held the East's wild-card spot until Atlanta beat Boston, leaving the Raptors behind on point differential, AP reports. They are 7-11 and host Denver on Sunday at 6pm ET. Ja'Kobe Walter left in the third quarter with a bruised right quad.

If you're new to this

The NBA has 30 teams and an 82-game regular season. Each team has five players on court. A shot scores 2 points, or 3 from beyond the arc, and a free throw scores 1.

The top six teams in each conference make the playoffs outright, and seeds 7 to 10 play a "play-in" tournament for the last two places. The playoffs are four rounds, each a best-of-seven.

There is a salary cap and a draft, which keeps spending closer than in most sports.

A triple-double means reaching 10 or more in three categories in one game, usually points, rebounds and assists. The NBA Cup is an early-season side tournament; its group games also count in the normal standings.
