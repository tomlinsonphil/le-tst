Raptors

Preseason, opener 21 Oct

The Raptors are warming up, with one of each so far. They lost the preseason opener 129–105 to Miami in Quebec City, the first NBA game in the city, with Kawhi Leonard held out, then bounced back with a 114–106 win over the Clippers in Vancouver.

RJ Barrett scored 20 and Immanuel Quickley 18 in Vancouver, with Collin Murray-Boyles adding 13 points and 9 rebounds off the bench. The regular season starts at home to the Chicago Bulls on Wednesday 21 October at Scotiabank Arena, with the Knicks visiting the next night, according to TSN.

If you're new to this

The NBA has 30 teams and an 82-game regular season. Each team has five players on court. A shot scores 2 points, or 3 from beyond the arc, and a free throw scores 1.

The top six teams in each conference make the playoffs outright, and seeds 7 to 10 play a "play-in" tournament for the last two places. The playoffs are four rounds, each a best-of-seven.

There is a salary cap and a draft, which keeps spending closer than in most sports.
