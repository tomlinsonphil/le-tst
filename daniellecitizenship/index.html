What this means for September 2027

Citizenship by Sept 2027 isn't possible. She can first apply in about July 2027, and processing took about 12 months in April 2026, so citizenship would come around mid-2028.

Moving in Sept 2027 is still possible if we move while her application is pending. What we understand of the rules (to be confirmed with IRCC or an immigration lawyer):

  • Days in Canada only have to be met in the 5 years before she applies. She can leave Canada after applying.
  • She must stay a permanent resident, keep a Canadian address (Toronto lease runs to Jan 2030), and answer IRCC mail promptly.
  • She has to attend the test, interview and oath. Some are offered online, otherwise she would fly back at short notice.
  • Keep Canadian ties and records of the move, in case IRCC asks about residence.
