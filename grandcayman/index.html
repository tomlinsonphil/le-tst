Getting around

Airport
A taxi to Seven Mile Beach is about US$25 for up to four, a 12 to 15 minute ride. Fares are set by the government, and the CI:GO app shows the fare.
Rides
Uber and Lyft don't operate here. Island Ride Cayman is the local app, and the host suggests it.
Car hire
Desks are a short walk from the arrivals hall, about US$40 to 100 a day. Drivers need to be 21 or over with a credit card, and traffic keeps left. Leading idea: hire from Thursday 10 to Sunday 13 for the north and west days, then return it at the airport on the way home. Not booked.

Before we go

Packing

Valuables, electronics, medication and passports stay in the cabin, not in checked bags.

Handy to know

Time
Same as Toronto in December. Cayman doesn't change its clocks.
Money
Cayman dollars, fixed at CI$1 to US$1.20. Shops take US dollars but give change in Cayman dollars. ATMs are everywhere.
Power
Same 120 V plugs and sockets as Canada.
Roads
Traffic keeps to the left.
Sundays
Most shops are closed.
Sun
Strong even in December. Reapply after swims.
Phones
WhatsApp calls are free over Wi-Fi, which the host recommends. Check the roaming plan before landing.
