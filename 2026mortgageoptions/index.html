4. Reading the results

  • Overpay before the switch. While on Virgin's SVR there is no early repayment charge, so a large one-off costs nothing to make. After the new deal starts, only about 10% a year is penalty-free.
  • The decision boils down to when you sell. A 2-year deal that ends around the earliest sale date limits the exit charge; a 3-year deal is cheaper to run but bites harder if the date arrives earlier than the term ends.
  • Ask the broker: does the rental income count towards Cambridge's UK-income test; is the overpayment allowance based on the initial loan or the outstanding balance; does Newbury's 2% early repayment charge taper?
  • Not modelled: UK capital gains tax on a sale (non-residents must report within 60 days), tax on rental profit, the month on Virgin SVR before the new deal starts, and any change in the Bank of England rate.
